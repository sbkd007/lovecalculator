How the FLAMES Love Game Works (Step by Step)

FLAMES is one of the oldest love calculation methods — a school playground classic that still trends on social media. Here is exactly how to play it:

  1. Write your full name and your partner's full name.
  2. Cross out every letter that appears in both names. Cross it out in both.
  3. Count the remaining uncrossed letters across both names.
  4. Write the word FLAMES (Friends, Lovers, Affection, Marriage, Enemies, Siblings).
  5. Starting from F, count to your number in a loop. Cross out the letter you land on. Restart counting from the next letter.
  6. Repeat until only one letter remains. That is your result.

For example, if Layla and Noah are the names: crossing shared letters (A, A) leaves L, Y, L, N, O, H — six letters. Counting six on FLAMES repeatedly, you would eliminate letters one by one until one remains.

The FLAMES method is entirely for entertainment but has a nostalgic charm that makes it universally popular across generations.

How to Calculate Love Percentage on Paper (2 Methods)

Method 1 — Letter Match Count

Write both names. Count how many letters appear in both names (shared letters). Divide the shared letter count by the total unique letters across both names, then multiply by 100. This gives you a rough percentage based purely on shared characters.

Example: Emma (4 letters) + Mark (4 letters) = 8 total. Shared letter: M and A = 2. Score = (2 ÷ 8) × 100 = 25%. Simple, but only a starting point.

Method 2 — Numerology Method

Assign each letter a number (A=1, B=2, ... Z=26). Add up the values for each name separately. Combine both totals and reduce to a single digit by adding individual digits until you reach a single number (1–9). Map that digit to a love percentage scale where 9 = 99%, 8 = 88%, and so on.

This numerology-based approach has roots in ancient traditions and tends to produce more varied, meaningful results than a simple letter match.
